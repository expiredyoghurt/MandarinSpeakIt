# Pilot protocol (draft for v0.2). Not yet run. Adapt with your Mandarin teacher and your school's research or data rules.

**Purpose:** find out whether the app is understandable, whether the content is right, and what the voice scoring does with real Singapore pupils. It is not an assessment of pupils.

1. **Before anything:** a Mandarin-teaching colleague reviews `docs/RECORDING_CHECKLIST.md` (every word and sentence), the pinyin shown, the unit order and the measure-word and sentence-order answers. Fix what they flag first.
2. **Consent:** school or parent consent for the pilot. Voice stays OFF for everyone until a parent has said yes AND you switch it on for the class.
3. **Group:** 6 to 10 pupils across P5 and P6, mixed Chinese confidence. Use first names or nicknames only.
4. **Sessions:** three sessions of about 12 minutes over a week, adult watching, no coaching on answers. Note where pupils hesitate, tap the wrong thing twice, or ask what an instruction means.
5. **Voice test (separate, opt-in pupils only):** each pupil reads 10 words twice. Compare what the app said (correct / near / try) with what a Mandarin teacher says. Note every case the app was wrong. This is the data for calibrating the XP bands and the accent allowances (zh/z, ch/c, sh/s, an/ang, en/eng, in/ing).
6. **Tones:** the app does not score them. Have the teacher note tone errors separately, so you can judge whether tone scoring is worth building.
7. **Check after:** pupils' unit progress looks sensible (not stuck, not racing); no pupil was upset by a "Good try"; no step needed scrolling on the devices used (record the devices).
8. **Remove data:** after the pilot, remove pilot pupils on the teacher page if you do not need them. Check the removal worked.

Record: device and browser, what happened, what you changed. Bring it to the next version's planning.
